Trout Trot · Android and iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Trout Trot does not collect, share or sell any personal information. The only things the game saves are the round you've reached, the charms you've earned and your play stats, and they stay on your phone.

What the game keeps on your phone

Trout Trot remembers the round you've reached so you can pick up where you left off. It also keeps the charms you've earned (small bonuses you earn as you go) and a few play stats for the ending screen: rounds cleared, flops, times lost and time played. The round and the charms are saved together, and all of it is saved in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere. Start over on the start screen clears them, and so does uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone's settings.

What the game doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Internet use

The game runs entirely on your phone and doesn't send anything over the internet. On Android, the app's listing shows a standard internet permission because the app is built on Android's web view, but the game doesn't use it.

Children

Trout Trot doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date at the top.
